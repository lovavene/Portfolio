About

What you should know about me

With this portfolio I want to show you who I am as a designer and as a person. I love colour, expression and to make a statement. To make this statement into an oxymoron, I also enjoy structure and clear guidelines such as brand guides and clear client requests.

As a designer, I value experiences where I get to work with others, creating solutions together and being part of a team. I am driven by the fact that there is always something new to learn and that there is always room to get better.

Originally from Gothenburg, I am currently finishing my bachelor’s degree at Jönköpings Tekniska Högskola, set to graduate in the spring of 2027.
