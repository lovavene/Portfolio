About

What you should know about me

With this portfolio I want to show you who I am as a designer and as a person. I love colour, expression and to make a statement. To make this statement into an oxymoron, I also enjoy structure and clear guidelines such as brand guides and clear client requests.

As a designer, I value experiences where I get to work with others, creating solutions together and being part of a team. I am driven by the fact that there is always something new to learn and that there is always room to get better.

Originally from Gothenburg, I am currently finishing my bachelor’s degree at Jönköpings Tekniska Högskola, set to graduate in the spring of 2027.

Hard Skills

Graphic Design is the main topic of my BA. I’ve been trained in visual identity, layout, typography, colour through design theory and practical work both for educational and commercial purposes . This is a skill that I have learned through different situations. Through my education I have received the necessary theory needed to make consious choices to create accessible, responsible, responsive design. Through education and working with clients I have been trained in moving this from theory to actual digital products. This skill is a practice that I thoroughly enjoy. During my semester abroad, as well as during internship and freelance work I have improved my abilities in Branding and Identity. Client work has been the main factor in improving this skill. . I use the various Adobe programs in the majority of my projects. I am the most proficient in Illustrator, but I have recieved proper introductions and used Ps, Id, Ae and LrC in multiple projects in the last 3 years. As my BA is part Web Dev, I have a working knowledge in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. This is knowledge I reuse in most Digital Products when it comes to structure, actual coding, working with the developers or learning new tools. I often tend to combine this with my knowledge in UX/UI. . Figma is a skill I have improved greatly in and use for almost every single one of my projects, be it for a digital product or for more graphic work. For Digital Product Development I use figma to make high fidelity wireframes, testing various animations and trying out different connection flows, all before it is translated into Code, Framer, Webflow or however the product is made. For more graphical projects, I often use Figma to collect all of my thoughts and guidelines and make a complete verision of what I am creating. This way, when I am to create it in Adobe, I have a blueprint for what to make. .

Soft Skills

As a creative and as a team member one of my main skills is my ability to follow the red thread in a project. My semester abroad further developed this skill and encouraged my ability of stepping back, combining ideas and seeing the bigger picture. Considering all parts of a process, from financial constraints, development skills and a timeframe, and adjusting what is to be done to these factors. Through my international educational experiences I have worked with many people different people, in different constellations and for different projects. I have through this seen first hand how good a product can become when combining people of different backgrounds. . Through educational and commercial experiences, I have worked first hand with clients. With this I have practiced communicating with clients, pitching receiving feedback and pivoting based on said feedback as well as working with briefs. Many of my experiences have differed drastically, different teams, different clients and different clients, and in this I know my own capabilities when placed in new situations. Because of this I can confidently say that I am an adaptable person, who can work with most people and in different constellations. This skill Is more of a combination of many of the ones listed. Through my experience and my way of working with the red thread, I would say I am good at looking at the problem in front of me, the constraints placed by the instance and the creative direction provided by the client to create solutions that meet the necessary criteria. Through my education, I would say I have a working knowledge in what AI is and how to use it to reach certain goals. Based on the same experiences, I have knowledge negatives sides to AI. Because of this, I feel I know when and how to use AI for the tool it is, and when to step away from it, with the aim of preserving IP, the creative process and the human touch.

Education

In the Spring of 2027, my Bachelor’s degree is completed, bringing to a close three years of learning. This education has taught me of Graphic Design Theory, Marketing and communications, The many steps of developing digital products, how to manage projects and consequently shown me what I am capable of. My 5th term of my BA was spent in Copenhagen at EK - Erhvervs­akademi Køben­havn, formerly known as KEA. Here i took the 30 ECT course Digital product development, which covered different methods of developing a product individually and as a team. The course covered the Google Design Sprint, Scrum, The startup way and Deep dive. Each project was made with a real client and followed their graphical and project descriptions. During my 3rd Term, I decided to take an extra course, and international blended intensive course combining students from Sweden, Belgium, Germany and the Netherlands. This course was focused on the importance of media literacy and the threats of AI, as well as the ethical aspects at play when designing with AI.
