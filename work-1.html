A Magazine Concept

CHAOS

What is Chaos?

Chaos Magazine is a fictional magazine concept created with original expression in mind. It illustrates many of my favourite ways of designing, being bold, loud, creative and colourful. Chaos is punky, edgy and is a magazine that covers up and coming artists. The concept consists of a front page as well as one composed spread and shows for how the magazine would cover a artist interview.

Photostory

With this project, I made the decision to work with stock photography to be able to convey the editorial look which I was Aiming for. This photo-story is sourced from Pexels, and the images portray the raw, edgy and authentic feeling that Chaos Magazine would.
